# Review axes

Evaluate only axes supported by the provided packet:

1. Contribution and prior-work distinction.
2. Importance and audience relevance.
3. Study design, experimental unit, controls, and bias.
4. Analysis validity, uncertainty, robustness, and multiplicity.
5. Claim-to-result alignment and alternative explanations.
6. Reproducibility, methods detail, data/code/material availability.
7. Figure/table integrity and cross-section consistency.
8. Ethics, consent, safety, conflicts, authorship, and research integrity.
9. Readability, terminology, structure, and accessibility to the intended audience.

Use severity based on consequence:

- `Major, Blocking Yes`: central claim cannot be established without resolution.
- `Major, Blocking No`: materially affects validity, scope, or interpretation but a bounded case remains.
- `Minor`: local clarity, reporting, or presentation correction that does not alter the central inference.
