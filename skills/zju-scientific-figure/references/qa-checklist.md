# Figure delivery QA

## Scientific

- Panel evidence supports the bounded conclusion.
- Experimental and sampling units are not confused.
- `n`, uncertainty, tests, multiplicity, and exclusions match the analysis.
- No bar, axis, normalization, smoothing, or crop creates a misleading comparison.
- Representative images have a documented selection rule and scale bar.

## Visual

- Every label is legible at final size.
- Colors, symbols, units, decimals, capitalization, and panel IDs are consistent.
- Legends do not rely on color alone.
- No text or mark is clipped, hidden, or outside the canvas.
- Adjacent panels align and whitespace communicates grouping.

## Files

- Editable source, data/transformation manifest, final exports, and legend exist.
- Fonts are embedded or safely substituted.
- Vector text remains selectable where appropriate.
- Raster images meet the current target journal specification, verified at delivery time.
