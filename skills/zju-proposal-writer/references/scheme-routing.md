# Scheme and stage routing

- `funding_call`: use the current official call, template, evaluation criteria, and budget instructions supplied or verified for that cycle.
- `opening_report`: emphasize question, literature basis, hypothesis, methods, feasibility, schedule, and degree requirements supplied by the program.
- `doctoral_plan`: separate training objectives from scientific objectives and record supervision/resources without inventing commitments.
- `internal_seed`: prioritize bounded proof-of-concept, decision gate, resource fit, and follow-on path.
- `collaborative`: record partner roles, data/material/IP interfaces, dependencies, and letters still required.

Modes:

- `compose`: build all foundation artifacts from a topic and evidence packet.
- `revise`: diagnose the supplied proposal before rewriting; preserve compliant content and track changes.
- `audit`: return findings and remediation without silently changing the proposal.

If the official scheme is unavailable, provide a generic draft marked `template_pending`; do not label it submission ready.
