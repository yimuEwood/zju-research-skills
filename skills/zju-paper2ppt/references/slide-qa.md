# Slide QA

- Timing fits the speaking slot with a buffer.
- Every slide has one legible claim and a traceable source anchor.
- Numbers, signs, significance, sample sizes, labels, and terminology agree with the source.
- Figures retain relevant panels, legends, scale bars, and attribution.
- Text is readable from the back of the intended room; no overflow or clipped objects exist.
- Color is accessible and not the only encoding.
- Speaker notes explain transitions, uncertainty, and likely questions.
- Claims based only on abstracts or OCR are marked.
- The final PPTX opens, uses the requested aspect ratio, and contains the expected slide count.
