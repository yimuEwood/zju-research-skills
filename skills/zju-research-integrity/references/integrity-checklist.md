# Research integrity checklist

## Evidence and analysis

- Raw data, metadata, exclusions, transformations, code, environments, and outputs are preserved and linked.
- Experimental and sampling units, missing data, multiplicity, stopping rules, and deviations are documented.
- Negative, null, contradictory, and failed results relevant to the claim are not hidden.

## Images and figures

- Original images exist; adjustments and crops are recorded and scientifically consistent.
- Duplications, splices, selective fields, scale bars, labels, and representative-image selection are explainable.

## Writing and credit

- Citations support the exact claim and are verified.
- Text/data/image reuse and overlap are disclosed and permitted.
- Author/contributor roles, acknowledgments, conflicts, funding, and AI/tool use match applicable rules and actual work.

## Governance

- Ethics, consent, biosafety, clinical registration, data/material agreements, export controls, and licenses are valid for the work performed.
- Confidentiality and personal data are minimized.

Record rule source, evidence, status, owner, remediation, and verification date for each applicable check.
