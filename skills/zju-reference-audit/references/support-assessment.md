# Citation support assessment

Evaluate one atomic claim at a time.

1. Split compound sentences into factual units: population/system, intervention or exposure, direction, magnitude, uncertainty, timing, and causal language.
2. Locate the relevant source passage, table, figure, supplement, or dataset.
3. Compare scope: species/cohort/material, conditions, outcome, direction, magnitude, and study design.
4. Assign one status:
   - `direct`: the cited evidence supports the full scoped claim.
   - `partial`: only part of the claim is supported; specify the unsupported part.
   - `context_only`: relevant background but not evidence for the asserted result.
   - `contradicted`: source evidence conflicts with the claim.
   - `not_assessable`: full evidence was unavailable or ambiguous.
5. Record an anchor and suggest the smallest safe wording change.

Causal claims require a design capable of supporting causal inference. Statistical significance does not establish practical importance, mechanism, or generalizability.
