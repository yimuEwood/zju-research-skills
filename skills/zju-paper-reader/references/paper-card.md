# Deep Paper Card

Use these headings:

1. `Research question and gap`
2. `Core contribution`
3. `Study system and design`
4. `Methods that determine interpretation`
5. `Argument spine`
6. `Claim-evidence map`
7. `Primary quantitative findings`
8. `Robustness, contradictions, and alternative explanations`
9. `Limitations and boundary conditions`
10. `Reproducibility assets`
11. `Reuse for my research`
12. `Evidence-synthesis handoff`
13. `Source anchors`

The argument spine maps each central author claim to method, evidence, warrant, alternative explanation, and boundary. `Reuse for my research` must distinguish reusable method, testable hypothesis, transferable dataset/code, and speculation. Preserve upstream record IDs in the evidence-synthesis handoff.
